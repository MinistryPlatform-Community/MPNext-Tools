Copy this block into `DECISIONS.md` under the matching `## <Domain>` heading (and add a TOC line); one section per decision. Never renumber or delete an ADR — supersede it, or append an `**Amended YYYY-MM-DD:**` line.

```markdown
### ADR-NNN: <title>

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** Accepted | Superseded by ADR-XXX | Superseded (YYYY-MM-DD) — <reason>
**Context:** why the question came up
**Decision:** what we chose
**Consequences:** tradeoffs / downstream constraints
**Alternatives considered:** other options + why rejected
```
